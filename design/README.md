# Mobius 3D icon explorations

The project was called Clayweld while these were made; the names below are from then.

`10-warm-gradient.png` was the app icon until 0.5.3. The icon is now a render of the viewer's own sample knot; `tools/icon/README.md` says how it is made.

Source: `../logo-reference.png`. These are visual concepts for review, not final app assets. The monochrome images are raster previews in a vector-like style; a selected mark can be redrawn as SVG for production.

`clayweld-icon-grid.png` presents nine treatments of the interlocked knot:

1. Pearl on charcoal
2. Frosted ice
3. Cobalt enamel
4. Satin titanium
5. Warm copper
6. Opalescent glow
7. Powder blue
8. Dark graphite
9. White on black

`clayweld-warm-pair.png` presents the first warm direction and its initial monochrome mark. All images were made with the built-in ImageGen tool using the supplied logo as the structural reference. `assemble_grid.py` rebuilds the review sheets from the local PNGs.

`clayweld-warm-pair-v2.png` is the current review pair. Its corrected monochrome mark, `12-monochrome-clean.png`, removes the detached white wedge from the upper-left opening and uses broader, smoother bands. This is a raster PNG concept, not an editable SVG.

## Latest prompts

**Warm rendered icon:** Use the user's Clayweld 3D interlocked knot logo as reference; faithfully preserve its three-lobed continuous topology, viewpoint, and over-under crossings. Isolate it from the screenshot. Render a dimensional knot on a near-black charcoal rounded-square tile. Saturated crimson red on upper surfaces flows to vivid orange through the middle and returns to rich red on lower surfaces, with only a narrow amber-gold transition. Smooth enamel, soft studio highlights, strong silhouette at 32 pixels. No text, flames, extra symbols, or watermark.

**Matching monochrome mark:** Use the warm icon as the edit target. Preserve its exact silhouette, proportions, loop openings, and crossing positions. Convert the knot to a pure white flat vector-style shape on pure black; use black negative-space cuts to clarify crossings. No gradients, shadows, textures, bevels, border, tile, gray, color, text, or watermark.
